Attention  
Potential Bidders 
  
Dear Sir/Madam,  
 
DT Global supports the implementation of the Australia Indonesia Partnership for Justice Phase 3 
(AIPJ3). AIPJ3 is a five-year program funded by the Australian Department of Foreign Affairs and Trade 
(DFAT) to work in partnership with the Government of Indonesia (GOI) to support advancement of its 
priority law and justice reforms and contribute to the overall goal of “Strong and accessible justice and 
security institutions uphold the rule of law and contribute to stability and prosperity in Indonesia and 
the region”.  
 
AIPJ3 has the following end-of-program outcomes (EOPOs): 
• EOPO 1: Justice institutions develop and implement policies, plans, budgets and practices 
that advance the rule of law and economic development in the priority areas of accountability 
and transparency, criminal and commercial law reform 
• EOPO 2: Justice institutions and partners implement and adapt policies, plans, budgets and 
practices that contribute to preventing violent extremism and addressing transnational crime  
• EOPO 3: Justice institutions develop and implement policies, plans, budgets and practices 
that advance equal access to justice for women, children and persons with disabilities 
• EOPO 4: Government of Indonesia, Government of Australia and non -government 
organisations deepen partnerships in the justice and security sectors for mutual benefit. 
 
To support the implementation of the AIPJ3 Communication Strategy, AIPJ3 plans to develop an official 
website as a key  platform for communicating the program’s  identity, messages, and values that 
increase visibility (awareness) toward the program.  The website also serves as a platform to document 
and disseminate AIPJ3’s progress, learning, and impact with the aim of strengthening positive 
understanding and perception of the program among relevant stakeholders and broader audiences.   
 
AIPJ3 is seeking a qualified subcontractor to design, develop and maintain the  AIPJ3 website .  
Interested organizations are invited to submit their technical and financial proposals along with all 
supporting documentation specified in this Request for Proposal, no later than 26 August 2026 at 
23:59 Jakarta time (GMT +7).  
 
 
Sincerely, 
 
 
Endang Dewayanti

Attachment 3 – Scope of Services 
 
Terms of Reference 
AIPJ3 Website Development and Maintenance 
 
A. Background 
AIPJ3’s website is an official channel of partnership’s external communications where AIPJ3 
Communications Team has full control of the narrative following DFAT’s approval mechanism. The 
website will also be a media to guide target audience to: 
1. Take further actions (read, download, share, provide feedback) in increasing the partnership’s 
visibility and positive sentiment to access information equally as it will be available in English, 
Bahasa Indonesia, and with accessibility features for people with disabilities.  
2. To get connected with AIPJ3’s stakeholders and civil society partners throughout  
3. Project’s lifecycle and draw attention from potential collaborators and networks. 
 
B. The Objective  
AIPJ3’s communication objectives for website 
a. To serve as an official platform to communicate AIPJ3’s identity, messages, and values that 
increase visibility (awareness) toward the program.   
b. To document and disseminate AIPJ3’s progress, learning, and impact that lead to more 
positive sentiment (perception) toward the program 
 
Key objectives for website providers are:  
1. Deliver a modern, responsive, bilingual website built to current standards and best practices. 
2. Ensure full compliance with WCAG 2.2 Level AA accessibility guidelines. 
3. Achieve strong Core Web Vitals scores (LCP, INP, CLS) to support organic search 
performance. 
4. Support content updates on weekly basis  
5. Integrate analytics, consent management, and privacy-compliant tracking 
6. Establish a secure, scalable hosting environment with documented maintenance and support  
7. procedures including regular content uploads.  
8. Complete all phases within the agreed timeline and budget. 
 
C. Scope of Work 
1. Design Concept (15 September – 15 October 2026)  
The design concept stage establishes foundation for the website. Activities include: 
• Client and stakeholders meeting to capture business requirements, user needs, and 
content Strategy. 
• User Experience (UX) research including persona development and user journey 
mapping.  
• Information architecture design — sitemap, content hierarchy, and navigation taxonomy. 
• Low-fidelity wireframes for key page templates (home, interior, contact, landing pages). 
• Brand and visual direction alignment, including typography, colour palette, and design 
language documentation  
• Formal sign-off for development stage
